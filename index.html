From discovery to decision

An Oopbuy shopping guide built around better questions.

An Oopbuy product list can help organize options. The useful work happens when you compare the exact item, the seller's information and the conditions of your purchase.

Think in total cost

Item price + domestic delivery + service/payment fees + international shipping + applicable taxes or duties.

  1. Choose a category before a brand.

    Start with the item you need: everyday sneakers, a warm layer, a jersey or a small accessory. Compare function and fit before narrowing by appearance.

  2. Record the exact listing and variant.

    Save the product URL, selected color, size and seller information. Similar photos or titles do not establish that two listings describe the same item.

  3. Compare measurements, not size labels alone.

    For clothing, compare chest, shoulder and length measurements with a garment you own. For shoes, use the seller's chart and confirm how any length measurement was taken.

  4. Inspect photos with a specific checklist.

    When researching Oopbuy QC photos, look for the selected variant, visible workmanship and relevant measurements. Lighting can alter color; photos alone cannot prove authenticity.

  5. Confirm the terms before paying.

    Verify current fees, shipping options, return conditions and any item restrictions with the relevant provider. Keep your order details and correspondence together.

Clear answers before you browse

The useful questions.

Understand what this guide provides and what still needs checking.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize shopping research. Depending on its creator, it may include categories, seller links, prices, sizing notes and quality-control references. A spreadsheet entry alone does not verify an item's quality, authenticity or availability.

Does this page contain a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category directory and shopping guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS category pages or the CAFANS homepage.

How should I compare shoe sizes?

Check the seller's size chart, confirm the measurement method and compare the stated measurements with footwear that fits you. Do not assume EU, UK and US size labels are interchangeable across sellers.

Can QC photos prove authenticity?

No. Quality-control photos can help you inspect visible details such as color, shape, labels and workmanship, but photos alone do not establish authenticity.

What costs should I compare before ordering?

Compare the item price, domestic delivery, service or payment fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Confirm current amounts and policies with the relevant provider before paying.

Is this an official Oopbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent guide linking to CAFANS. It does not claim affiliation with Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.
